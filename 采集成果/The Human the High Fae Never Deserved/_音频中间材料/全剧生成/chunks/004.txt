Emma: My iron sickness. When I was thrown out of the celebration, I got hurt so Kastar made—

Toros: You’re still going on with that iron sickness deershit? You’re just a weak human. Don’t blame it on an iron deficiency and make our nurse do extra work to take care of you.

Emma: I… didn’t ask her to make that…and I can’t help that humans need iron to live.

Kellan: You know iron burns our skin. So selfish to force your way into our world and try to make us feel guilty that you’re so weak.

Toros: I still say it’s fake. You’re being pathetic. Toros tosses the vial carelessly at Emma, who hurries to catch it.

Kellan: That’s your plan to get better? Please. No Fae would ever choose you as a mate!

Toros: You know she’s been trying to get mom to force one of us to be her mate. Can you imagine being strapped to her for her entire human life span?

Emma: I would never make someone be my mate if they didn’t want to. Toros and Kellan turn to leave.

Kellan: Save it, Emma. We don’t want to hear your lies anymore. And you better stay away from Mara. If I hear that you’re giving her a hard time, I won’t let it go. The door slams shut, leaving Emma alone in the infirmary. A tear slips down her cheek. She drops her head, staring at the vial.

Emma: Should I bother taking this? Or should I let myself waste away until they finally believe me? A magically-spelled letter slips under the door and flies through the air, a trail of dark red smoke follows its path before it lands in her hands.
