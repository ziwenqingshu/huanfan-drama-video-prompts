Mara: Don’t make her, Kellan. I’ll be fine. Emma promised to leave me alone if I give her her room back. I just need to go grab my things. Mara takes a step forward but loses balance and the brothers rush forward to restabilize her.

Toros: You will not be moving out of that room!

Kellan: Yeah, we won’t let a lowly human bully a pure-blood fae like this. Especially not one as good as you, Mara. Come on, we’ll help you get upstairs. The brothers help Mara up the stairs, one on each side of her, holding an arm. As they pass Emma, Toros purposely moves his shoulder to shove her aside. This knocks her backwards. Mara throws a smirk at Emma over her shoulder. Emma stares at her, mouth hanging open. Once they’re gone, Emma doesn't move. She wipes at a tear on her cheek, looking lost. Then a magical letter floats on a trail of amber smoke and golden sparkles and lands in her hands. Embossed across the front of the envelope is: “From Queen Raina”.

Mara回头对Emma投去一抹讥笑。Emma则呆立原地，嘴巴张得老大。他们离开后，Emma便一动不动。她擦去脸颊上的一滴泪水，神情迷茫。随后，一封魔法信件伴随着琥珀色的烟雾与金色的光点飘浮着降落在她手中；信封正面印着：“来自Queen Raina”。

外景：恩贝尔梅尔城堡——晚霞

镜头首先定格在恩贝尔梅尔城堡——金色的壁炉中燃着温暖的火焰，将余烬洒向暮色中的天空；头 顶星空闪烁，此时夜色已悄然降临。通往城堡庭院的浅褐色雕刻石门上设有多个开放式拱门，通 向一座露台；露台上摆放着盆栽，其中柠檬树挂满了果实。轻纱窗帘为众多开放式窗棂勾勒出轮 廓，随风轻轻摇曳；金色的光芒在空中飘荡。

内景：恩贝尔梅尔城堡——Raina的书房——黄昏

Queen Raina端坐在恩贝尔梅尔城堡的客厅里的一张书桌前，身着一件缀满无数钻石的庄重蓝色长袍。 她用一支魔法羽毛笔写信。
