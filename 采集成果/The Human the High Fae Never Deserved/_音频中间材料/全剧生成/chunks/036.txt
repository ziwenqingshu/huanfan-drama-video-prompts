Toros: Enough, Kellan! Emma, please, I’m begging you. Don't we mean anything to you anymore?

外景：恩贝尔梅尔学院-前门入口-上午时段-连续

Emma: Because he chose me! Adrian believed me without question. He has shown me true kindness, and I feel safe with him.

我不接受！ Toros释放出他的力量，向Emma投出一根嗡嗡作响的绳索。这根绳索完全缠绕在Emma的身上， 将她向自己拉去。Emma因剧痛而尖叫起来——这股能量引发了剧烈的痛苦。 Adrian疾速冲入战场，双臂挥动出一道宽广的火焰束。他利用这道火焰切断了来自Toros 的能量连接——这股能量在Emma周围骤然停滞，随即反向轰击向Toros，致使他踉跄后退、 失去平衡。

Toros: I don’t accept that! Toros unleashes his power, sending a buzzing cord toward Emma. It wraps completely around her body, pulling her toward him. Emma screams in pain, the energy causes intense pain.

Adrian: Touch my mate again, and you’ll regret it. Toros and Kellan look shocked, then they lift their arms like they are going to start a battle of the elements with Adrian. Toros’s energy lights up on his hands, and Kellan calls more water from the fountain.

外景：恩贝尔梅尔学院-前门入口-上午时段-连续

Adrian站在Toros与Emma之间，Kellan则站在Toros身旁，位于那道深紫色大门前。Toros的手 上迸发出阵阵能量，而Kellan则从学校场地后方的喷泉中引出一股宽阔的水流。Toros与Kellan均 穿着德里文家的便服——由于经历了三天的马车之旅，他们的衣着显得有些凌乱、不修边幅； Emma则身着恩贝尔梅尔学院的校服。Emma正喘着气，因疼痛而皱着眉头。其他students则在一旁观 看着这场热闹场面。 Toros继续积蓄力量，怒吼着，准备对Adrian发动猛烈攻击。而Adrian则让 双手间迸发的火焰逐渐消退，随后便直视着Toros，未予还击。

Adrian: I’ll only give the warning once, no one harms my mate.

Student 1: Did Prince Adrian just claim Emma is his mate?

Emma随即站直了身子——在摆脱了Toros的能量网的影响后，她已缓过气来。

Emma: I’d like to officially introduce you both to my mate, Prince Adrian Embermere.

Toros: No! I don’t believe it!

Kellan: You couldn’t get one of us to be your mate, so you tricked him?

Toros: She should have been mine! Toros races toward Adrian, ready to unleash his force on him. Adrian engages a shield of fire, but then drops it as soon as Toros is near, jukes to to avoid being hit, then uses a maneuver to use Toros’s forward motion against him, causing Toros to tumble to the ground. A cloud of dust puffs up as he lands. Kellan immediately tries to attack Adrian, but Adrian dodges him, ducking low and gets his weight underneath Kellan, then flings him off. Kellan lands flat on his back on the ground with a thud.

Toros落地时激起一阵尘雾。Kellan随即试图攻击Adrian，但Adrian轻松闪避——他低身躲闪，从Kellan身下借力，将他掀翻出去。Kellan重重地摔在地面上，背部着地，发出沉闷的声响。

Adrian与Emma并肩而立；Toros与Kellan则倒在地上，站在深紫色大门前。两人均穿着德里文家 的便服——因经历了为期三天的马车之旅而显得衣衫不整、杂乱无章，又因被抛到地上而浑身污 秽；Emma则身着恩贝尔梅尔学院的校服。其他学生则从背景处注视着这一幕。
