Emma伸手触碰着医务室床边的小桌，从中取出一封魔法信件和一支魔法笔。她一拿起信件，信件边缘便立刻散发出缕缕白光。她随即准备开始书写。

Emma: It’s clear I can’t stay here any longer. Maybe Queen Raina at the Twilight Fae Court will help me. She favored my mom and has often told me I’m welcome there.

Emma: Dear Queen Raina, I’ve decided to finally accept your invitation to come stay with you and Prince Adrian at Embermere Castle. I’d like to come as soon as possible. Emma folds the letter, which illuminates with magic. It flies out the window, a trail of white smoke and yellow sparkles in its wake.
